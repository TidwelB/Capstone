SCP: Fallen

Demo Video

Enter the mysterious laboratory where danger lurks around every corner as you try to make your way out through this eerie adventure horror game. Complete puzzles and search for items to help you get away while gruesome creatures try to drag you back in. Will you be able to complete the challenges, evade the entities, and escape the lab before it’s too late?

Easy to play!

SCP: Fallen is the perfect game for new gamers, old gamers, and even returning gamers! Using traditional style controls SCP: Fallen helps players that have already played games feel right at home. While the extensive tutorial is perfect for new gamers to learn how!.

Replayable!

Thinking hard, running fast, and keeping your eye on your back. SCP: Fallens random generated puzzle and speed run elements make it a perfect game for replaying. Attempting to beat it in a shorter time than your first play-through is all part of the appeal!

Horror!

Want to be scared out of your pants?! Look no further as SCP: Fallen uses lighting and sneaky mechanics to intimidate and terrify the player
